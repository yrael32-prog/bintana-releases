Bintana (beta)

Translucent desktop windows that keep your installed games one click away: Steam, Xbox / Game Pass, game folders and anything you drag in. Free during the beta, with no ads.

Download the latest beta (Windows 10/11, 64-bit)

Privacy in one line: Bintana sends an anonymous daily usage count (a random install number, the app version and edition) so we know how many people use it. Nothing about you or your games ever leaves your PC. You can turn it off during installation or any time in Settings. Privacy notice

This site also hosts the app's update feed and licence status list.
