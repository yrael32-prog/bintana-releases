Game Dock privacy notice (beta)

Plain-language notice for the free beta. A full privacy policy will replace it before version 1.0.

What stays on your PC

To show your games, Game Dock reads Steam's library files, the folders you choose to watch, Xbox app install folders, and the icons of the games and shortcuts it shows. None of this ever leaves your PC.

What Game Dock sends

  • Anonymous daily usage count (on by default; turn it off in the installer or in Settings › General): once a day, a random install number created on first run (not derived from your PC, accounts or games), the app version and edition, and how many times each ad was shown and clicked (there are no ads during the beta). We use it only to count active users and, from 1.0, to report ad performance in aggregate.
  • Update check (once a day if enabled in Settings, or when you ask): downloads a small file listing the latest version, and the installer if you choose to update.
  • Licence status (founder/paid licences): about once a day, downloads a list of deactivated licence numbers and checks yours against it on your PC. Your key is never sent.

Like any web request, these reach our servers with your IP address and the app version. There is no other analytics, telemetry or crash reporting, and nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

What is stored on your PC

Settings, window layout, cached icons, copies of shortcuts you drop onto windows, logs and your licence key are stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\GameDock. Uninstalling removes the program and asks whether to delete this folder too.

Purchases

Optional founder/support purchases are handled by the store you buy from (for example itch.io), under its own privacy policy. We receive your email and order details only to send and support your licence key.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/yrael32-prog/gamedock-releases.
